Everything about your expenses, on your device

📄 Receipts your way

Add receipts with photos, PDFs or text files. Scan paper receipts with the camera and auto-crop them.

🤖 On-device OCR

Scan a receipt photo and Re-Seat fills in the merchant, amount, tax, currency and date — privately, with no network.

📊 Reports

Group receipts into reports like "Holidays 2026" and see totals by category. Your name goes on every report.

🚗 Mileage Allowance

Enter from, destination, distance (km or miles) and a rate per unit — the total is calculated for you.

💶 Multi-currency

Set your base currency and edit exchange rates. Totals convert automatically.

📤 Export & backup

Export any report as CSV or ZIP, and back up everything to a single file you keep.

See it in action

Private by design

All data — receipts, photos, PDFs, text files and your details — lives in a single database on your iPhone. OCR scanning runs on-device. No accounts, no tracking, nothing uploaded. You can back up and restore your data yourself.
